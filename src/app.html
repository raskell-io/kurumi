Kurumi: a local-first, AI-native second brain

Kurumi is a personal knowledge app. Capture notes, voice memos, links, and meetings, then find them again through wikilinks, backlinks, a graph view, full-text search, and an AI assistant.

It runs in your browser, works offline, and installs as an app. There is no account, your data stays on your device, and sync to storage you choose is optional.
